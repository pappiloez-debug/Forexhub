How to Use a Trading Journal Dashboard

A trading journal dashboard provides a structured way to review historical trades. Instead of looking at individual trades separately, a dashboard can summarize important statistics such as the number of trades, winning trades, losing trades, win rate and net profit or loss.

The EZTradingHub Trade Journal Dashboard is designed to work with trade information recorded through the EZTradingHub trading journal. It can help organize historical performance information for educational review.

Review Your Win Rate

Win rate represents the percentage of recorded trades that were profitable. A higher win rate does not automatically mean that a trading strategy is profitable, because the size of winning and losing trades also matters.

Review Average Wins and Losses

Comparing average winning trades with average losing trades can provide additional context when reviewing historical performance. Two trading approaches can have similar win rates while producing very different results because their average win and average loss sizes differ.

Study Trading Streaks

Winning and losing streaks can help traders examine the sequence of their historical results. Streak information should be interpreted carefully because a historical sequence does not guarantee that the same sequence will occur again.

Review Your Best and Worst Trading Days

Looking at daily results can help organize a performance review. Traders may choose to examine whether certain market conditions, sessions, instruments or strategies appeared frequently on their strongest or weakest days.

Why Keep a Trading Journal?

A trading journal creates a record of the decisions and results associated with individual trades. Useful journal information can include the instrument, direction, entry, exit, stop loss, target, position size, profit or loss, strategy and notes about trading psychology.

How Your Journal Data Is Used

This dashboard is designed to calculate statistics from trade records stored in your browser. The dashboard does not need a server-side database to calculate the displayed statistics.

Because browser storage can be cleared by the user, browser data should not be treated as a permanent backup. If your trading journal provides an export function, regularly export important records and keep your own backup.

Avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, account login information or other highly sensitive personal information into a trading journal.

Trade Journal Dashboard FAQ

Common questions about the EZTradingHub trading performance dashboard.

What is a trade journal dashboard?

A trade journal dashboard summarizes recorded trades using statistics such as total trades, winning trades, losing trades, win rate, profit and loss, trading streaks and monthly performance.

Where does the dashboard get my trades?

The dashboard reads trade records saved locally by the EZTradingHub trading journal in your browser. The dashboard uses those records to calculate the displayed statistics.

Can I use this dashboard on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop devices.

Does the dashboard provide trading signals?

No. The dashboard summarizes historical journal information. Its statistics are not trading signals or predictions of future market movements.

Is this dashboard financial advice?

No. The dashboard is an educational record-keeping and performance-analysis tool and does not provide personalized financial or investment advice.
