What Is a Prop Firm Payout Calculator?

A prop firm payout calculator helps traders estimate how much money they may receive from eligible trading profits after applying an assumed profit split.

For example, if a hypothetical funded account generates $2,000 in eligible profit and the trader's assumed profit share is 90%, the mathematical trader share would be $1,800.

The actual amount paid by a proprietary trading company can depend on account type, payout schedule, minimum requirements, consistency rules, fees, drawdown conditions and other provider-specific terms.

Important: This calculator provides estimates. It is not an official payout calculator for any proprietary trading company.

How Does a Prop Firm Profit Split Work?

A profit split determines how eligible trading profits are divided between the trader and the proprietary trading firm.

A simplified 90/10 profit split means:

  • Trader share: 90%
  • Firm share: 10%

If eligible profit is $1,000:

Trader: $1,000 × 90% = $900

Firm: $1,000 × 10% = $100

The exact arrangement depends on the provider's current agreement and account type.

Prop Firm Payout Formula

The basic formula used by this calculator is:

Eligible Profit = Total Profit × Payout Percentage

Gross Trader Payout = Eligible Profit × Trader Split

Firm Share = Eligible Profit × Firm Split

Net Trader Payout = Gross Trader Payout − Fees

These formulas provide a straightforward estimate and do not automatically incorporate every provider-specific condition.

Example: $10,000 Funded Account Payout

Consider a hypothetical $10,000 funded trading account with $2,000 in eligible profit and a 90% trader profit share.

  • Account size: $10,000
  • Trading profit: $2,000
  • Trader split: 90%
  • Firm split: 10%
Trader gross payout:
$2,000 × 90% = $1,800

Firm share:
$2,000 × 10% = $200

If there were no additional fees and the entire $2,000 were eligible for payout, the simplified trader payout would be $1,800.

What Is an Eligible Profit for Payout?

Eligible profit is the portion of account profit that satisfies the provider's payout conditions.

Depending on the specific trading program, eligibility may depend on factors such as:

  • Minimum trading days
  • Minimum profit requirements
  • Maximum drawdown conditions
  • Daily loss requirements
  • Consistency rules
  • Payout schedule
  • Account type
  • Verification or compliance requirements
  • Provider-specific trading restrictions

The calculator allows you to enter a percentage of profit to analyze because actual eligibility rules vary.

Prop Firm Payout vs Account Balance

A funded account's displayed balance and the amount a trader can withdraw are not necessarily the same thing.

The account may contain unrealized profit, realized profit, required buffers or other amounts that are subject to the provider's rules.

Before requesting a payout, traders should review the provider's current rules for available profit and any required account buffer.

Planning tip: Consider tracking both your total account profit and the amount that is actually eligible for withdrawal.

Prop Firm Payout and Drawdown

Payouts and drawdown should be analyzed together when managing a funded account.

Withdrawing profit can change the account balance or equity reference used by a provider. Depending on the firm's rules, this may affect available drawdown or other account metrics.

Never assume that a payout has the same effect across all prop firms. Check the specific rules for your account type.
