How the Market Scanner Works

The scanner combines price data with basic technical calculations to help organize markets for further analysis.

RSI Analysis

The Relative Strength Index is a momentum oscillator commonly calculated over 14 periods.

RSI ≥ 70 = commonly considered overbought
RSI ≤ 30 = commonly considered oversold
RSI 31–69 = neutral range used by this scanner

RSI is not a standalone prediction tool. Strong trends can remain above or below commonly used RSI thresholds for extended periods.

Moving Averages

The scanner calculates a 20-period SMA and a 20-period EMA from the selected timeframe.

Price > SMA + Price > EMA → Bullish bias

Price < SMA + Price < EMA → Bearish bias

When price is between the moving averages, the scanner classifies the condition as neutral.

Market Scanner FAQ

What is the EZTradingHub Market Scanner?

It is a market-analysis tool that organizes financial instruments according to live or latest available price data and technical conditions.

Can I scan forex markets?

Yes. The scanner includes commonly monitored forex pairs such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD and USD/CAD.

Can I scan gold?

Yes. XAU/USD is included in the default instrument list. Availability depends on the connected market-data provider.

Can I scan cryptocurrency?

Yes. BTC/USD, ETH/USD and other selected cryptocurrency pairs can be included in the scanner.

Does the scanner use RSI?

Yes. RSI is calculated from the historical closing prices returned by the market-data provider.

Does the scanner use moving averages?

Yes. The scanner calculates a 20-period simple moving average and exponential moving average.

Are the results guaranteed trading signals?

No. Technical indicators are analytical tools and do not guarantee future price movements or trading results.
