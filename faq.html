General Questions

What is EZTradingHub?

EZTradingHub is a website providing trading calculators, financial calculation tools and educational resources covering areas such as forex, gold, cryptocurrencies, stocks, commodities, bonds and ETFs.

Who is EZTradingHub for?

EZTradingHub is designed for people who want to understand or perform common trading and investment calculations. This includes beginners learning financial concepts as well as experienced market participants looking for convenient calculation tools.

Is EZTradingHub a broker?

No. EZTradingHub is not a broker, exchange or investment management company. It provides educational information and calculation tools.

Does EZTradingHub provide financial advice?

No. EZTradingHub provides general educational information and calculation tools. The website does not provide personalized financial, investment or trading advice.

Calculator Questions

What trading calculators are available?

EZTradingHub offers calculators covering areas such as pip values, position sizing, trading risk, profit and loss, margin, leverage, drawdown, risk-to-reward, compounding, forex, gold, crypto, stocks, commodities, bonds and ETFs.

Are EZTradingHub calculators free to use?

EZTradingHub provides online calculators and tools through the website. Features and availability can vary between individual tools as the platform develops.

Can beginners use EZTradingHub calculators?

Many calculators are designed with straightforward inputs and explanations so beginners can learn how common financial calculations work. Understanding the underlying concept is still important before using a result for a real-world decision.

Are calculator results guaranteed to be accurate?

Calculator results depend on the information entered, the calculation method and the assumptions used. Market prices, spreads, commissions, fees, contract specifications, interest rates and other conditions may change. Important figures should therefore be independently verified.

Do EZTradingHub calculators work with all brokers?

The calculators are intended for general calculations. Different brokers can use different contract sizes, margin requirements, spreads, commissions, leverage limits and other specifications. Users should verify broker-specific information before trading.

What information do I need to use a calculator?

The required inputs depend on the calculator. Common inputs may include account balance, entry price, stop-loss price, take-profit price, position size, risk percentage, leverage, fees or other relevant market information.
