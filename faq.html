What is a pip? +

A pip is a standard unit used to measure price movement in forex. Most major currency pairs use 0.0001 as one pip, while many JPY pairs use 0.01.

How much is one pip worth? +

The value of one pip depends on the currency pair, position size, exchange rate and account currency.

How much is 1 pip on 0.01 lot? +

For a typical USD-denominated EUR/USD position, 0.01 lot is approximately $0.10 per pip. The exact value can vary depending on the instrument and exchange rate.

How much is 50 pips worth? +

Multiply the pip value by 50. For example, if one pip is worth $1, then 50 pips represents approximately $50 before trading costs.

Does lot size affect pip value? +

Yes. Increasing the position size generally increases the monetary value of each pip.

Are pip values the same for every currency pair? +

No. Pip values can differ because of the currency pair, exchange rate, position size and account currency.

Does the calculator guarantee my actual trading result? +

No. Calculator results are estimates. Your broker's contract specifications, spread, commission, swap, execution price and exchange rates can affect the actual result.

What is a good risk percentage per trade? +

There is no universal percentage that is appropriate for every trader. The amount someone should risk depends on their strategy, financial circumstances, account size and risk tolerance.

What is the 1% trading rule? +

The 1% rule is a risk-management approach where a trader limits planned loss on an individual trade to approximately 1% of their trading account.

How much should I risk on a $1,000 account? +

At 1% risk, the planned monetary risk would be $10. At 2%, it would be $20. This does not mean either percentage is appropriate for every trader.

Does a stop-loss guarantee my maximum loss? +

No. In fast-moving or illiquid markets, slippage and gaps can cause actual losses to exceed the intended stop-loss amount.

Does leverage affect my risk? +

Leverage can increase the size of positions a trader can control, but actual planned monetary risk also depends on position size, stop-loss distance and the instrument being traded.

Can I use this risk calculator for gold? +

You can use the risk-management principles for gold, but gold/XAUUSD has its own contract specifications and price behavior. The exact calculation should account for your broker's specifications.

Why is risk management important? +

Risk management is an important part of trading because financial markets can move against a position. Managing position size, stop-loss levels, and potential exposure can help traders control the amount they are willing to risk.

What is a lot size? +

Lot size represents the size of a trading position and affects how much each price movement is worth.

What is 0.01 lot? +

For many standard forex pairs, 0.01 lot is commonly referred to as a micro lot and represents approximately 1,000 units of the base currency.

How do I calculate lot size? +

Lot size can be estimated using your planned monetary risk, stop-loss distance and pip value per lot.

What lot size should I use for a $100 account? +

There is no universally appropriate lot size. It depends on the instrument, stop-loss distance, account currency, broker specifications and the amount you are willing to risk.

Does a larger lot mean more risk? +

Generally, yes. For the same instrument and price movement, a larger position generally produces a larger monetary gain or loss.

Can I use this lot-size calculator for XAUUSD? +

The risk-management approach can be applied to gold, but you should use the contract specifications for your particular XAUUSD instrument and broker

Does leverage determine my lot size? +

Leverage affects the margin required to open a position, but risk-based position sizing also depends on factors such as account balance, stop-loss distance and pip/tick value.

How do I calculate forex profit? +

Forex profit depends on the direction of the trade, entry price, exit price, position size, contract specification and relevant exchange rate.

How do I calculate profit on a buy trade? +

For a simplified calculation, subtract the entry price from the exit price and apply the position size and relevant contract value.

How do I calculate profit on a sell trade? +

For a simplified calculation, subtract the exit price from the entry price and apply the position size and relevant contract value.

Does lot size affect profit? +

Yes. A larger position generally produces a larger monetary gain or loss for the same price movement.
