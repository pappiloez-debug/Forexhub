Why Use a Trading Dashboard?

Traders often use several different tools when planning and reviewing trades. A dashboard can make those resources easier to find by organizing them in one location.

The EZTradingHub Dashboard provides navigation to calculators covering areas such as forex, commodities, cryptocurrency, stocks, bonds, ETFs and risk management.

The calculations provided by individual tools depend on the inputs supplied by the user. Results should therefore be treated as estimates for educational and planning purposes rather than guaranteed financial outcomes.

Basic Trading Risk Management Principles

Risk management involves understanding how much capital could potentially be exposed to a trade or series of trades. Different markets and brokers can have different contract specifications, margin requirements and trading conditions.

Position Size

Position size refers to the quantity of an instrument being traded. The appropriate calculation depends on factors such as account size, entry price, stop-loss distance, contract specifications and the trader's selected risk parameters.

Stop Loss

A stop-loss level can be used as part of a predefined trade-management plan. It does not eliminate market risk, and execution may differ from the selected level in some market conditions.

Risk-Reward Planning

Risk-reward calculations compare a hypothetical amount at risk with a potential target. The ratio alone does not determine whether a trade or strategy will be profitable.

Frequently asked questions about the EZTradingHub Dashboard.

What is the EZTradingHub Dashboard?

Can I use the dashboard on my phone?

Yes. The dashboard uses a responsive layout designed for smartphones, tablets and desktop computers.

Are the calculators financial advice?

No. EZTradingHub calculators are educational tools intended to help users explore calculations and hypothetical scenarios. They do not provide personalized investment recommendations.

Are calculator results guaranteed to be accurate?

Calculator results depend on the information entered by the user and the assumptions used by the particular calculator. Broker specifications and market conditions may differ, so important results should be independently verified before being used for financial decisions.

Does EZTradingHub provide trading signals?

The dashboard is primarily designed to provide educational resources, calculators and record-keeping tools. Users should not interpret calculator outputs as guaranteed trading signals or predictions.
