Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EZTradingHub ("EZTradingHub", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the EZTradingHub website and its trading calculators, tools, Trade Journal, Dashboard, and related pages.

1. Information We Collect

EZTradingHub is designed primarily as a free, browser-based collection of trading calculators and tools. We do not need your name, password, bank details, or trading account credentials simply to use the calculators.

Some tools may store information locally in your browser, such as saved preferences, calculator history, favorites, or journal/dashboard information. This information may remain on your device until you clear the relevant browser storage. We do not describe locally stored information as being transmitted to us unless a particular feature actually sends it to a server.

2. Information You Provide

If EZTradingHub later provides a contact form, account registration, newsletter, or another feature that asks you to submit personal information, we will use the information for the purpose for which it was provided and explain the applicable use at the point of collection.

Please do not submit passwords, private trading-account credentials, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive financial information through an ordinary contact message or calculator field.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

EZTradingHub may use browser storage technologies, including local storage, to remember certain features and preferences. Browser storage is different from server-side storage and may be controlled through your browser settings.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added to EZTradingHub, those services may use cookies, pixels, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies according to their own policies. We will update this policy when applicable services are introduced.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

EZTradingHub may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to help support the free availability of the website. If Google advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising.

Important: Advertising technologies may allow third parties to place and read cookies in a visitor's browser and may use information such as IP addresses or identifiers in connection with ad serving. The exact technologies and advertising choices available to visitors can change according to the services enabled and applicable law.

Google provides information about how it uses data when advertising is displayed on publisher websites. You can review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users can manage Google's advertising personalization choices through Google Ads Settings.

5. Analytics and Performance Services

EZTradingHub may use analytics or performance-monitoring services in the future to understand general website usage, improve page performance, and identify technical problems. If such a service is enabled, the relevant provider and applicable data practices will be reflected in this policy.

6. Third-Party Links

EZTradingHub may contain links to third-party websites, including trading platforms, financial services, educational resources, advertising partners, or other external services. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and information handled through features we control. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

EZTradingHub is not designed as a service for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices

You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing site data may remove locally saved calculator preferences, history, favorites, or journal/dashboard information.

Where applicable, you may also use the privacy and advertising controls provided by third-party services such as Google.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when EZTradingHub introduces new features, advertising, analytics, or other services, or when applicable requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when the policy is materially updated.

11. Contact

If you have a privacy question or concern about EZTradingHub, please use the official contact method provided on our Contact page.
