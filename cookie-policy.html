1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, maintain functionality, understand how visitors use a website, and support advertising or measurement activities.

Cookies may be session-based, meaning they expire when your browsing session ends, or persistent, meaning they remain on your device for a specified period or until they are deleted.

2. How EZTradingHub May Use Cookies

EZTradingHub may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping the website functioning properly.
  • Remembering certain user preferences.
  • Supporting website security and performance.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with our pages.
  • Measuring website traffic and engagement.
  • Supporting advertising and ad measurement.
  • Improving website content, navigation, and user experience.

3. Types of Cookies and Similar Technologies

Essential Technologies

These technologies may be necessary for certain website features, security functions, navigation, or basic functionality.

Preference Technologies

These may remember choices such as display preferences or other settings so that the website can provide a more consistent experience.

Analytics Technologies

Where analytics services are used, cookies or similar technologies may help measure traffic, page usage, and general website performance.

Advertising Technologies

Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, personalize, or limit advertisements, subject to applicable settings and requirements.

4. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

EZTradingHub may use browser technologies such as local storage in addition to traditional cookies.

For example, the website's light/dark theme preference may be stored locally on your device so that your preferred display mode can be remembered when you return to the website.

Local storage is different from cookies, but it can perform similar functions in certain situations.

5. Theme Preference

EZTradingHub may use browser local storage to remember whether you selected the light or dark appearance of the website.

This preference is intended to improve usability and does not require you to create an account.

Clearing your browser's site data may remove this saved preference.

9. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • Block all cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Allow cookies only from selected websites.
  • Receive notifications when cookies are being used.
  • Clear website data and local storage.

Blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect the functionality or appearance of some websites.

10. Browser Settings

You can generally manage cookies through the privacy or security settings of your web browser.

The exact controls vary between browsers and devices. For detailed instructions, consult the official support information for the browser you use.
