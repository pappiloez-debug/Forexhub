1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can help websites remember preferences, maintain functionality, understand website usage, and provide certain services.

2. How EZTradingHub Uses Cookies

EZTradingHub may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies for different purposes.

These purposes may include:

  • Essential website functionality.
  • Remembering your cookie-consent preferences.
  • Understanding how visitors use the website.
  • Measuring website performance.
  • Supporting advertising and advertising measurement.

3. Types of Technologies We May Use

Necessary Technologies

These technologies are required for essential website functionality and security.

They may also be used to remember your consent preferences.

Analytics Technologies

Analytics technologies may help us understand visitor interactions, traffic patterns, popular pages, and website performance.

These technologies are optional and may only be activated after the appropriate consent is provided.

Advertising Technologies

Advertising technologies may be used to display, measure, or personalize advertising where applicable.

These technologies are optional and may only be activated after the appropriate consent is provided where required.

4. Third-Party Services

EZTradingHub may use third-party services such as analytics providers, advertising networks, affiliate platforms, embedded content providers, or other service providers.

These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The exact third parties used by EZTradingHub may change as the website develops.

5. Your Cookie Choices

Where consent is required, you can choose whether to allow optional analytics and advertising technologies.

You can select:

Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can review or change your cookie preferences at any time.

6. Browser Settings

You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or appearance of some websites.
