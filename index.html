Trading Risk & Money Management Resources

Calculators are more useful when combined with sound risk management and clear trading rules.

Why Position Size Matters

Position sizing connects the amount you are willing to risk with the distance to your stop loss. A smaller stop does not automatically mean a smaller monetary risk because position size also changes.

Traders should verify pip value, tick value, contract size, spread, commissions and account-currency conversion before placing a trade.

Prop Firm Risk Management

A proprietary trading evaluation can have specific daily loss and maximum drawdown requirements. These rules can be different between providers and account types.

A funded account risk calculator can help model scenarios, but it should not replace the provider's official rules.

Gold Trading Risk Management

XAUUSD can have different contract specifications and value conventions from major forex pairs. Always check the exact symbol specification provided by your broker.

Gold traders can combine the gold risk, gold lot size and gold margin calculators to review different parts of a trade before execution.

Funded Account Drawdown Planning

Drawdown planning involves understanding how much loss is available before an account reaches its applicable threshold. Trailing drawdown and equity-based rules can require special calculations.

Treat calculator results as estimates and confirm the exact calculation method for your account.
