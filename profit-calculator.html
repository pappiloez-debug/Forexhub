What Is a Forex Profit Calculator?

A forex profit calculator estimates how much a trading position could gain or lose based on the price at which the trade is opened, the price at which it is closed, the trade direction and the position size.

It can be useful before entering a trade to understand potential outcomes and after a trade to review its performance.

The calculated result is an estimate. Actual trading results can differ because of spreads, commissions, swaps, slippage, exchange rates and broker-specific contract specifications.

How Buy and Sell Trades Affect Profit

Buy Trade

A buy trade generally profits when the market price increases after the position is opened. The basic price movement is calculated as the exit price minus the entry price.

Sell Trade

A sell trade generally profits when the market price decreases after the position is opened. The basic price movement is calculated as the entry price minus the exit price.

Forex Profit Calculation Formula

For a simplified calculation:

Buy Profit = (Exit Price − Entry Price) × Position Size
Sell Profit = (Entry Price − Exit Price) × Position Size

The exact monetary result depends on the instrument, contract size, account currency and applicable exchange rate.

Forex Profit Calculator Example

Suppose a trader buys EUR/USD at 1.1000 and closes the position at 1.1050.

  • Direction: Buy
  • Entry price: 1.1000
  • Exit price: 1.1050
  • Price movement: 0.0050

The position has moved in the trader's favor. The actual monetary profit depends on the position size and the contract specifications of the trade.

What Happens When a Trade Loses?

A loss occurs when the market moves against the trading position. For a buy position, this generally means the exit price is below the entry price. For a sell position, the exit price is above the entry price.

Understanding potential losses is just as important as estimating potential profits. Traders should consider their stop-loss, position size and planned account risk before entering a trade.

Profit Potential and Risk Management

Estimating potential profit should not be separated from risk management. Traders can compare a potential target with their planned stop-loss to evaluate the risk-to-reward relationship of a trade.

A larger potential profit does not automatically mean a trade is better. Position size, probability, market conditions and the amount being risked should also be considered.

Can the Profit Calculator Be Used for Gold?

Gold trading, commonly represented by XAUUSD, can also involve calculating potential profit or loss from price movement and position size.

However, gold contract specifications can differ from standard forex currency pairs. Traders should verify the contract size, tick size, tick value and minimum position size provided by their broker.

Can I Calculate Crypto Profit?

Cryptocurrency trades can also involve calculating the difference between an entry price and exit price multiplied by the amount of the asset held.

Crypto exchanges can use different contract, fee and settlement structures, so actual results may differ from a simple calculator estimate.
