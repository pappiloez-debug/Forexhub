How to Use a Prop Firm Challenge Calculator

  1. Enter your challenge's starting balance.
  2. Enter the current balance and any available current-equity figure.
  3. Use the profit target percentage specified by your provider.
  4. Enter the daily loss limit and its correct reference balance.
  5. Enter the maximum drawdown percentage and the profit recognized toward your target.
  6. Optionally enter the number of remaining trading days.
  7. Calculate and review each result against the provider's current rules.
This calculator assumes a static maximum drawdown measured from starting balance. It does not model trailing drawdown, intraday equity-based thresholds, different daily reset times, consistency rules, news restrictions or other provider-specific conditions.

Example: A $10,000 challenge with an 8% profit target

If the starting balance is $10,000 and the target is 8%, the target amount is $800. The corresponding target balance is $10,800, assuming the provider calculates the target from starting balance and measures the recognized result in the same way.

Understanding daily loss limits

A daily loss limit is a threshold for losses over a provider-defined trading day. The reference point and reset time matter. Some programs may consider equity, floating losses, commissions or swaps. Enter the actual reference balance and loss already used today to estimate the remaining allowance.

Understanding maximum drawdown

Maximum drawdown limits restrict how far an account may fall under the provider's calculation method. A static limit based on initial balance differs from a trailing limit that can move as the account reaches new highs. Confirm which approach applies to your challenge.

Prop Firm Challenge Formulas

Profit target

Target amount = Starting balance × Target percentage ÷ 100

Target balance

Target balance = Starting balance + Target amount

Remaining profit

Remaining profit = Target amount − Recognized profit accumulated

Daily loss threshold

Daily loss threshold = Daily reference balance × Daily loss percentage ÷ 100

Static maximum drawdown floor

Static floor = Starting balance − Maximum drawdown amount

These formulas are simplified planning calculations. A provider may use a different reference balance, include unrealized losses, adjust the threshold daily, or apply additional restrictions.

Planning for FundedNext and Other Funded Trading Programs

Traders researching FundedNext and other proprietary trading firms can use this calculator to understand the arithmetic behind targets and loss thresholds. It is not an official FundedNext calculator and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FundedNext.

Do not rely on percentage values from another challenge

Different challenge types and account sizes can have different profit targets, daily limits, total drawdown rules, trading-day conditions and other requirements. Use the terms for the exact program you have selected rather than assuming that a percentage from another account applies to yours.

Plan for fees and floating losses

Trading commissions, spreads, swaps and unrealized losses may affect your account measurements. If your provider includes these in its loss calculations, make sure your planning accounts for them.

Use a separate trade risk calculation

Challenge-level thresholds do not determine the appropriate lot size for an individual trade. Consider stop-loss distance, pip value, trading costs and exposure from other open positions when calculating position size.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a prop firm challenge calculator?

It is a planning tool for estimating a challenge's profit target, daily loss threshold, maximum drawdown, remaining target amount and progress from user-entered figures.

2. Can I use this for FundedNext challenges?

You can use it for general calculations while researching a FundedNext challenge. The tool does not automatically load or verify FundedNext's current rules. Check the terms for your specific program.

3. How much profit is an 8% target on a $10,000 account?

An 8% target on $10,000 is $800. If the provider calculates the target from the initial balance, the target balance would be $10,800.

4. What is a daily loss limit?

It is a provider-defined threshold for losses during a trading day. Its calculation may depend on the reference balance, equity, floating P/L, costs and the provider's daily reset time.

5. What is maximum drawdown?

Maximum drawdown is a limit on how far an account can fall under a program's rules. Some programs use a static threshold while others use a trailing or equity-based method.

6. Does this calculator support trailing drawdown?

No. The drawdown floor displayed here is static and calculated from starting balance. A trailing threshold requires additional information about the provider's specific rules and account history.

7. Does this tool guarantee that I will pass a challenge?

No. It only performs calculations from the inputs you provide. Trading outcomes and compliance depend on your actions, market conditions, execution and the rules of the program.

8. Why should I enter current equity?

Current equity can differ from balance when trades are open. Comparing equity with the static floor can provide an additional reference, but it does not replace the provider's actual drawdown calculation.

9. Is this calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser and does not require a paid subscription or broker connection.
