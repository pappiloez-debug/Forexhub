Understanding Pips in Forex Trading

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in the foreign-exchange market.

Standard Forex Pairs

For many non-JPY currency pairs, one standard pip is commonly represented by 0.0001 of the quoted exchange rate.

JPY Currency Pairs

For many Japanese-yen currency pairs, one standard pip is commonly represented by 0.01.

Pipettes

Some brokers quote forex prices to an additional decimal place. A fractional pip is often called a pipette.

Price Difference

The price difference is the ending price minus the starting price. Dividing that difference by the pip size converts it into pips.

Bullish Movement

When the ending price is higher than the starting price, the price movement is positive or bullish.

Bearish Movement

When the ending price is lower than the starting price, the price movement is negative or bearish.

Pip Difference Formula

The basic calculation is straightforward when the correct pip size is known.

Pip Difference = (Ending Price − Starting Price) ÷ Pip Size

For example, if a forex pair moves from 1.10000 to 1.10500 and the pip size is 0.0001:

(1.10500 − 1.10000) ÷ 0.0001 = 50 pips

The calculator also reports the absolute pip movement so that the size of the move can be reviewed independently from its direction.

Pips vs. Monetary Profit

Pip movement and trading profit are related but they are not the same measurement.

Pip Movement

Measures how far the market price moved according to the selected pip convention.

Pip Value

Represents the monetary value of one pip for a particular position, depending on the instrument, position size and account currency.

Position Size

Larger or smaller positions can change the monetary impact of the same number of pips.

Approximate Profit/Loss = Pip Difference × Pip Value

This relationship is simplified. Actual trading results can also be affected by spread, commission, swaps, execution price, contract specifications and currency conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about pips and price movement.

What is a pip?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in forex. For many major currency pairs it is 0.0001, while many JPY pairs use 0.01.

How do I calculate pips between two prices?

Subtract the starting price from the ending price and divide the result by the appropriate pip size.

What is a pipette?

A pipette is commonly used to describe a fractional pip. For example, on a five-decimal forex quote, one pipette can represent 0.00001.

Does 100 pips always equal the same amount of money?

No. The monetary value of 100 pips depends on the currency pair, position size, pip value and account currency.

Can I use this calculator for XAUUSD?

You can use the calculator for price differences when you know the appropriate price-movement unit for your broker and instrument. Gold contracts can use different point or tick conventions, so verify the specification provided by your broker.

Is the pip difference the same as profit?

No. Pip difference measures price movement. Profit or loss depends on position size and the monetary value of the price movement.
