What Is a Pip?

A pip is a standard unit used to describe a small change in the exchange rate of a forex currency pair. For many major currency pairs, one pip is equal to 0.0001 of the quoted price.

Japanese yen pairs are commonly quoted with two decimal places, so their conventional pip size is generally 0.01. Some brokers also quote fractional pips, sometimes called pipettes.

How Does a Pip Calculator Work?

A pip calculator estimates the monetary value of a pip based on the position size, instrument and account currency.

For many USD-quoted forex pairs, a simplified calculation for a standard lot is:

Pip Value = Lot Size × Contract Size × Pip Size

The result may need to be converted into the account currency when the quote currency and account currency are different.

Why Pip Value Matters in Forex Trading

Knowing the monetary value of each pip can help traders understand how much their position may gain or lose when the market moves. It can also be useful when planning position size and evaluating potential trade risk.

  • Estimate potential profit or loss.
  • Understand the financial impact of a stop-loss.
  • Compare different lot sizes.
  • Plan trades using a defined risk amount.
  • Understand how currency-pair movements affect account value.

Example: Pip Value Calculation

Suppose a trader opens a 0.10-lot EUR/USD position. For a simplified example where one standard lot has a pip value of approximately $10 per pip, a 0.10-lot position would have a pip value of approximately $1 per pip.

A 20-pip favorable movement would therefore represent approximately $20 before spreads, commissions, swaps and other trading costs.

Pips vs Points

The terms pip and point are sometimes used differently by brokers. A pip generally refers to the conventional standardized price movement of a currency pair, while a point can refer to a smaller price increment or the minimum quoted movement.

Always check your broker's symbol specifications before relying on a pip or point calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pip in forex?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in forex. For many major currency pairs it represents 0.0001 of the quoted exchange rate.

How much is one pip worth?

The value of one pip depends on the currency pair, trade size, pip size and account currency. For many USD-quoted major pairs, one standard lot is approximately $10 per pip.

How much is a pip worth on 0.01 lot?

For many USD-quoted major forex pairs, 0.01 lot is approximately $0.10 per pip. Actual values can differ depending on the pair and exchange rate.

How much is a pip worth on 1 lot?

For many USD-quoted major currency pairs, one standard lot of 100,000 units has an approximate pip value of $10.

Can this calculator calculate XAUUSD?

The calculator includes an XAU/USD option, but gold contracts differ between brokers. Check your broker's contract size, tick size and point specifications before using the result for an actual trade.

Is a pip calculator the same as a profit calculator?

Not exactly. A pip calculator focuses on the monetary value of price movement, while a profit calculator can incorporate entry price, exit price, position size and other trade information.
