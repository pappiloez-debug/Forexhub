2. Trading and Investment Risk

Financial markets involve risk. Depending on the financial instrument and trading strategy, losses can occur rapidly.

Leverage and margin can increase both potential gains and potential losses.

Risk Warning

Never risk money that you cannot afford to lose. Before trading or investing, make sure you understand the relevant risks, costs, fees, leverage requirements, and product characteristics.

EZTradingHub provides calculators for purposes such as position sizing, pip calculations, profit and loss, margin, leverage, risk, return, bonds, stocks, commodities, ETFs, cryptocurrency, and other financial calculations.

Calculator results are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered by the user.

Actual results may differ because of factors including:

  • Broker or exchange specifications.
  • Spreads and commissions.

  • Market volatility.
  • Currency conversion rates.
  • Contract specifications.
  • Taxes and other applicable charges.
  • Rounding and differences in calculation methods.

Important calculations should be independently verified before being used for actual financial decisions.

4. Forex and CFD Trading

Forex and CFD trading can involve significant risk and may not be suitable for every person.

Broker conditions, contract sizes, margin requirements, spreads, commissions, swap charges, and other trading specifications can vary between providers.

Always verify the specifications of your particular broker or trading platform before placing a trade.
