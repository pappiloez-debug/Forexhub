What Is a Pip in Forex Trading?

A pip is a standard unit used to describe price movement in a currency pair. For many non-JPY forex pairs, one pip is 0.0001. For many JPY pairs, one pip is 0.01. Some platforms quote fractional pips, often called pipettes, as an additional decimal place.

Pip conventions for gold, silver, cryptocurrencies and indices may vary by broker and contract. For this reason, the pip size and pip value fields on this page are editable. Confirm the definitions used by your trading platform before relying on a result.

What Is Pip Value?

Pip value is the amount your position gains or loses when the market moves by one pip. It depends on the instrument, position size, contract specifications and account currency. A pip value quoted for one standard lot should not automatically be applied to a micro lot or to a different instrument.

Example: Calculating Pip Movement

Suppose EUR/USD moves from 1.08500 to 1.08700. The price difference is 0.00200. With a pip size of 0.0001, the movement is 20 pips. For a Buy trade, that movement is positive before costs. For a Sell trade, it is negative before costs.

If the pip value is $10 per pip for one standard lot and the position size is 0.10 lot, the estimated pip value is $1 per pip. A favorable 20-pip movement would therefore produce an estimated $20 gross profit before spread, commission, slippage and other charges.

Using Pip Calculations for Prop Firm Challenges

A proprietary trading firm challenge commonly includes rules related to profit targets, daily losses, maximum losses, or trading conduct. A pip calculator can help you estimate the financial effect of a price movement, but it does not determine whether a trade complies with a particular firm's rules.

1. Plan risk before entering a trade

Estimate the amount you could lose if price reaches your stop-loss. Compare that estimate with the amount you have chosen to risk on the trade. A stop-loss may execute at a different price during gaps or fast markets, so the final loss can exceed the estimate.

2. Understand the effect of position size

For the same instrument and pip value, a larger lot size generally creates a larger monetary gain or loss for each pip of movement. Position size should be considered together with stop-loss distance, trading costs, and applicable account limits.

3. Check your firm's actual drawdown rules

Daily-loss and maximum-loss rules are not identical across all providers or account types. Read the current terms for your specific challenge, including how equity, open trades, commissions, swaps, and the daily reset time are treated.

4. FundedNext and other funded account traders

Traders researching FundedNext or other funded-account programs can use this page for general pip and trade-value estimates. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official calculator for FundedNext or any other proprietary trading firm. Verify current program conditions directly with the provider.

Frequently Asked Questions About Prop Firm Pip Calculators

1. What is a prop firm pip calculator?

It is a tool for estimating price movement in pips and the associated monetary gain or loss for a trade. This calculator also estimates stop-loss risk using the pip value and volume you enter.

2. Can I use this calculator for FundedNext?

Yes. You can use it for general pip calculations while planning a FundedNext challenge or funded account. It does not access FundedNext accounts or automatically enforce their rules.

3. How many pips are in 20 pips of EUR/USD movement?

With a pip size of 0.0001, a price movement of 0.0020 equals 20 pips. For example, 1.0850 to 1.0870 is a 20-pip upward move.

4. Is a pip the same as a pipette?

No. A pipette commonly represents one-tenth of a pip on platforms that quote fractional pips. The exact number of displayed decimal places depends on the instrument and broker.

5. Does this calculator support XAU/USD gold?

Yes. Select Gold (XAU/USD), then confirm the pip size and pip value with your broker. Gold contract sizes and quoting conventions vary, so do not assume that every broker uses the same value per pip.

6. Does it calculate pip value automatically from live rates?

No. The pip value is entered manually. This avoids presenting a guessed value as a live conversion. Check your broker's specifications or use a suitable live pip-value tool for instruments that require currency conversion.

7. Can I calculate risk for a funded trading account?

Yes. Enter your account balance, risk percentage, pip value, lot size and stop-loss distance. The calculator estimates the planned risk and compares it with your selected risk budget.

8. Does this calculator guarantee that I will pass a prop firm challenge?

No. It performs calculations only. Trading outcomes, execution, fees, market conditions, and the rules of the provider all affect results. No calculator can guarantee a challenge outcome.

9. Why is my broker's pip value different?

Pip value can vary because of contract size, position size, exchange rates, account currency, and instrument specifications. Verify that the pip value entered on this page matches your broker's definition.

10. Is this prop firm pip calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be used directly in your browser without a paid subscription.
