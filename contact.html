Contact FAQ

A few quick answers before you contact us.

What can I contact EZTradingHub about?

You can contact us about website feedback, technical problems, trading calculator suggestions, general questions, partnerships, and other EZTradingHub-related inquiries.

Can I suggest a new trading calculator?

Yes. We welcome suggestions for new calculators, dashboards, trading tools, and useful features that could improve the EZTradingHub experience.

Does EZTradingHub provide financial advice?

EZTradingHub provides educational tools and information. Our calculators and resources are not a substitute for professional financial or investment advice.

How can I report an issue with a calculator?

Send us the name of the calculator, describe the issue, and include the inputs or circumstances that caused the problem. This helps us investigate the issue more efficiently.
