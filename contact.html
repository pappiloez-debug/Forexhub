Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about EZTradingHub.

Are the calculators free to use? +
Many of the tools available on EZTradingHub are designed to be accessible directly through the website. Check the individual calculator for its available features.
I found an error in a calculator. What should I do? +
Please contact us and explain the issue, including the calculator you were using and the values you entered. This helps us investigate the problem.
Can I suggest a new calculator? +
Yes. We welcome suggestions for new trading calculators and financial tools. Use the contact form and select "Tool Suggestion."
Does EZTradingHub provide financial advice? +
No. EZTradingHub provides calculators, educational resources, and informational content. Our tools are not intended to provide personalized financial or investment advice.
