Trading Calculator Guide

How to Use EZTradingHub Trading Calculators

Choose the calculator that matches the calculation you need. Enter the relevant values and review the result generated from those assumptions.

Trading calculators are particularly useful when you want to separate position sizing and risk decisions from emotions. For example, a trader can calculate the maximum amount they intend to risk before deciding whether a particular XAUUSD or forex setup meets their trading plan.

Using Calculators for Prop Firm Challenges

Traders preparing for a prop firm evaluation may use risk, position-size, drawdown, margin, profit and risk-reward calculators to model hypothetical trading scenarios.

A calculator does not determine whether a trade complies with a particular firm's rules. Daily loss limits, maximum drawdown, prohibited strategies, news restrictions, overnight requirements, leverage and other rules can vary between providers and account types.

Using Calculators for Funded Accounts

Funded-account traders can use EZTradingHub tools to estimate XAUUSD and forex risk, position size, pip movement, margin, drawdown and potential trade outcomes.

The result should be treated as an estimate based on the information entered into the calculator. Always compare the result with the current specifications of your broker or funding provider.

Gold trading requires particular attention to contract size, quotation precision, leverage, margin and volatility. EZTradingHub provides dedicated XAUUSD tools for gold pip calculations, gold risk, gold lot size, gold margin and gold trading-plan preparation.

Why Broker and Provider Specifications Matter

Trading instruments can have different contract sizes, tick sizes, pip conventions, margin requirements, spreads and trading conditions. Two platforms can therefore produce different monetary results from apparently similar calculations.
