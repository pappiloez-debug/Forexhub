Trading Education

How to Use Trading Calculators

Trading calculators are useful for working through numerical assumptions before making a trading or investment decision. Select the calculator that matches the calculation you want to perform and enter the relevant information.

For forex and leveraged trading, important inputs may include account balance, position size, entry price, stop-loss level, leverage, contract size and currency conversion. For investments, relevant inputs can include purchase price, current value, dividends, yield, fees and holding period.

Why Position Sizing Matters

Position sizing connects the amount traded with the amount of capital that could be exposed to a loss. A position-size or risk calculator can help organize these numbers before a trade is entered.

Why Trading Costs Matter

Trading outcomes can be affected by spreads, commissions, financing charges, execution prices, slippage, taxes and other costs. Calculator results may therefore differ from the final result of an actual transaction.

Broker Specifications Can Differ

Contract sizes, tick sizes, pip conventions, margin requirements, spreads and other trading conditions can vary between brokers and instruments. Always compare calculator assumptions with the specifications of the instrument and broker you are using.

Calculator Results Are Estimates

The calculators on EZTradingHub do not predict future market prices or guarantee investment results. Results depend on the assumptions and information entered by the user and should be interpreted accordingly.
