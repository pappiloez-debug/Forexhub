How to Use EZTradingHub Calculators

Select a calculator that matches the type of calculation you want to perform. Enter the relevant values and review the result generated by the calculator.

For trading calculations, it is important to use accurate account, instrument and trade information. Broker specifications can vary, particularly for contract sizes, margin requirements, spreads, commissions and other trading conditions.

Risk Management Matters

Trading calculators can help organize numerical information, but they cannot remove market risk. Consider your account size, position size, stop-loss level, leverage and potential loss before entering a trade.

Calculator Results Are Estimates

Results generated by these tools may depend on the assumptions and values entered by the user. Actual trading or investment outcomes can differ because of market movements, execution prices, fees, spreads, commissions, taxes, liquidity and other factors.
