How to Use EZTradingHub Calculators

EZTradingHub calculators are designed to make common trading and investment calculations easier to review. Select the tool that matches your task, enter the required information, review the result and check the assumptions used by the calculator.

Risk Management

Risk, lot size, position size, drawdown and risk-to-reward calculators can help users understand numerical exposure before making a trade. They do not determine whether a trade is suitable.

Forex, Gold and Crypto

Trading calculations can depend on contract size, leverage, spreads, commissions, exchange rates, liquidity and broker or exchange specifications. Always compare calculator assumptions with the specifications that apply to your account.

Stocks, ETFs and Fixed Income

Investment calculations are estimates based on the inputs provided. Actual results can differ because of fees, taxes, market prices, distributions, reinvestment and other factors.

Why Use a Calculator?

  • Reduce repetitive manual calculations.
  • Check risk and position assumptions before a trade.
  • Compare hypothetical scenarios using consistent inputs.
  • Understand how changes in price, size, return or cost affect a result.
  • Support your own research and financial planning process.

Are EZTradingHub Calculators Financial Advice?

No. The calculators provide mathematical estimates based on the information entered by the user. They are not personalized financial, investment, tax or trading advice and they do not guarantee future results.

Important: Calculator results are estimates for educational and informational purposes. Verify important calculations against your broker, exchange, fund provider, bank or other relevant financial institution before relying on them.
